    About CST Forum DPI

    A student community platform for Computer Science & Technology students.

    CST Forum DPI is a student-run community platform built for the Computer Science & Technology department of Dhaka Government Polytechnic Institute. It gives students a single place to ask questions, share resources, watch tutorials and showcase their work.

    • Ask and answer problems Post a problem with a photo or file attachment and get help from other CST students.
    • Resource center Books, notes, previous questions, lab sheets and syllabi — organised by semester.
    • Video center Tutorials, lab demonstrations and seminars shared by the department and students.
    • Project showcase Submit your project and let other students discover what you have built.
